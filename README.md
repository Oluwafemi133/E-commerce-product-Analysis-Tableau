# E-Commerce Product Analysis - 2000 Products | Category, Brand & Pricing Insights

![Dashboard](ecommerce_dashboard_final.png)

## 📊 Project Overview
Analyzed 2000 e-commerce products to uncover pricing, brand performance, customer rating, and discount strategies across 7 categories.

**Tools Used:** Tableau Public Desktop, Excel, Data Cleaning
**Dashboard Size:** 1200 x 800 (Custom)

## 📈 Dashboard Contains 4 Visuals:

### 1. Average Selling Price by Category
- Electronics: $52K (Highest) - Premium products
- Home: $17K, Sports: $13K
- Books & Grocery: $1K (Lowest)

### 2. Ratings Vs Total Reviews by Category
- Scatter analysis shows relationship between average rating and review volume
- Helps identify high-rated, high-trust categories
- Categories with >100 avg reviews have stable ratings

### 3. Top 10 Brands by Stock Quantity and Average Rating
- Plum (618.5), Decathlon (577.4), Nivea (576.1) - Highest Stock
- Indicates strong inventory and market demand
- All top brands maintain good stock levels above 518 units

### 4. Average Discount % by Category
- Grocery: 28.3% (Highest discount)
- Books & Electronics: 28.0%
- Beauty: 25.9% (Lowest discount - premium pricing strategy)

## 💡 Key Business Insights
1.  Electronics drives highest revenue per product but requires low discount strategy.
2.  Beauty category maintains premium pricing with lowest discount % (25.9%).
3.  Plum and Decathlon lead in stock availability, showing strong supply chain.
4.  Grocery uses high discount strategy (28.3%) to drive volume sales.
